# Casa Crobu Summer Market Capture Pilot Proposal

**Draft date:** April 2026  
**Purpose:** Turn the audit findings into a practical summer implementation plan, with clear phases, responsibilities, inputs, metrics, and pricing options.

## Executive Summary

Casa Crobu has proven market demand. The opportunity is to stop letting so much of that demand stay anonymous.

The recommended pilot is a three-phase summer plan:

1. **May + June: Prep + Capture Infrastructure**  
   Build the email/SMS capture system, QR paths, lid sticker CTA, consent handling, offer setup, and tracking foundation.

2. **July + August: Summer Experimentation**  
   Use the market list actively with weekly specials, pre-reserve tests, channel testing, offer learning, and a 45-minute weekly sync if I am supporting activation.

3. **September + October: Fall Delivery Conversion**  
   Wind down summer markets with segmented thank-you messaging, then use summer engagement data to launch fall delivery, freezer-stock, or recurring order tests.

This proposal separates:

* what I own,
* what Casa Crobu owns,
* what access or decisions are needed,
* which metrics matter in each phase,
* and pricing options depending on how much support Casa Crobu wants.

## Project Goal

Build a repeatable market capture and activation system that:

* converts anonymous market buyers into known email/SMS contacts,
* optionally links returning market buyers back to Shopify customer profiles at checkout,
* uses consented communication to build response habits during summer,
* learns which markets, products, and offers create engagement,
* and transitions the strongest signals into fall delivery demand.

## Implementation Appendix

### Source Of Record

The cleanest setup is to treat **Shopify as the source of record for customers, orders, POS activity, revenue, products, discounts, and market purchase history**.

The email/SMS platform, likely Klaviyo if the setup makes sense, should act as the **activation layer**: signup forms, consent capture, welcome flow, weekly perks, clicks, unsubscribes, and campaign engagement.

The dashboard should be the **reporting layer**, not the source of truth. It should pull from Shopify and the email/SMS platform so we can see what is happening without manually redefining the data.

| Data Type | Source Of Record | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer profile | Shopify | Shopify customer ID should become the durable customer record when possible. Email is the primary signup identifier; phone is optional for SMS. |
| POS orders + revenue | Shopify | Shopify should be the definitive record for purchases, discounts, products, and revenue. |
| Market-list tags/segments | Shopify + email/SMS platform | Tags/properties should sync where possible so customers are usable in both selling and messaging systems. |
| Email/SMS consent | Email/SMS platform, synced to Shopify where possible | Consent should be captured and maintained in the platform used to message customers. Do not message customers without the right consent status. |
| Campaign engagement | Email/SMS platform | Opens, clicks, replies, unsubscribes, and flow performance live in the messaging platform. |
| Weekly reporting | Dashboard | The dashboard summarizes results; it should not become the place where core customer/order data is manually edited. |

If there is a conflict, Shopify wins for orders/revenue and the email/SMS platform wins for message engagement and consent events.

### Member Lookup + POS Attribution Add-On

The key is not just getting someone onto the list. The next level is making sure future market orders can be tied back to that person.

This is valuable, but it can be treated as a separate Phase 1 add-on if Casa Crobu wants stronger attribution from the start.

The add-on would create a simple identity loop:

1. **Customer signs up from the market QR/lid sticker.**  
   The form should collect email as the primary identifier, with optional SMS consent. It should also tag the signup source, such as market, QR asset, or offer.

2. **That profile syncs into the customer system.**  
   If Klaviyo is the platform path, the goal is to sync name, email, phone, consent status, and market-list tags/properties into Shopify so the customer can be found in Shopify POS and included in Shopify customer segments.

3. **The welcome message teaches the customer how to redeem.**  
   Example: "Show this message or give your name/email at the booth so we can apply your market-list offer."

4. **Staff attaches the customer to the POS cart before checkout.**  
   This is the most important operational behavior. The offer should train the customer and staff to link the order to the customer profile.

5. **Every linked market order improves attribution.**  
   Once the customer is attached to the POS order, Casa Crobu can measure repeat market orders, offer redemptions, market-to-online conversion, and eventual fall delivery conversion.

If the customer redeems a generic discount but is not attached to the POS order, Casa Crobu may still see campaign-level redemption, but it will lose customer-level attribution. The staff prompt and checkout behavior are what make this data more valuable.
