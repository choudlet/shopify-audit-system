The Problem To Solve

Out of 32,330 POS orders, 29,621 are anonymous.

8.38% Known POS order share

Only 2,709 POS orders have an email or Shopify customer profile attached. This is not active booth capture yet.

What that means operationally

Casa Crobu cannot follow up with most market buyers, measure repeat behavior, learn which markets are working, or move shoppers into online ordering and delivery offers.

For anonymous buyers, we do not know who they are, how many times they came back, what they bought over time, or which offers would have brought them online.

How We Solve This

Build the system in four connected steps.

1 Capture

Lid Stickers + Market QR Codes give market shoppers a simple reason to identify themselves.

2 Consent

Collect email first, make SMS optional, and route new signups into Klaviyo with the right consent status.

3 Activate

Use the welcome flow and monthly summer offers to train the list to open, click, and redeem.

4 Convert To Delivery

Turn summer response data into personalized fall delivery bundles, freezer-stock boxes, and recurring order tests.

Consent + Activation

Build the funnel and activate it at the same time.

Audience Consent Handling First Message Next Step
Existing email-consented customers Port from Shopify into Klaviyo and segment by known behavior: market only, online only, market + online. Summer market kickoff: join the market list and tell customers what updates to expect. Monthly market offers and fall delivery transition.
Existing customers without marketing consent Do not add to marketing blasts. Re-permission through compliant opt-in at future checkout or market QR path. No marketing message until they opt in. Capture consent in the new funnel.
New market signups QR/tap-to-text form collects explicit email and/or SMS consent and tags source market. Immediate welcome message with the first offer or market-list confirmation. Segmented monthly offer and online/delivery follow-up.
SMS contacts Only message people with explicit SMS consent. Email consent does not equal SMS consent. Short confirmation with value, link, and STOP language. Weekly market specials, with or without offers, while monitoring opt-outs and reply behavior.
