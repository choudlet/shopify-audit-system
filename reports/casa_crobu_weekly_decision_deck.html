Email Or Texting At Launch

Do we want texting included from the beginning?

The Signup Page

Can collect text permission

The form can collect a phone number, record permission to text, add the customer to Shopify, and show the discount. It cannot send a welcome text by itself.

Shopify Messaging

Can send text campaigns

Casa Crobu can send occasional texts to customers who agreed to receive them. The current Shopify setup does not automatically send a welcome text after signup.

Another Texting Service

Adds automatic welcome texts

A paid texting service is needed if customers should text a word like PASTA to join or automatically receive a welcome text after signing up.

Decision for Kelly: start with automatic email and collect phone numbers for later, or add a paid texting service at launch?
