# Farmers Market Growth Service Playbook

## Positioning

**Service name:** Market-to-Web Growth Audit

**Promise:** Help market-based food and maker businesses turn in-person seasonal traffic into owned email/SMS lists, repeat web orders, and better market-by-market operating decisions.

**Best-fit clients:**

* Shopify or Square POS vendors who also sell online.
* Prepared food, frozen food, bakery, beverage, coffee, flowers, body care, pantry, or specialty CPG brands.
* Businesses doing multiple farmers markets per week with inconsistent list capture.
* Brands with repeatable products, seasonal drops, pickup/delivery/preorder options, or subscription potential.

## Why The Timing Works Now

Many Colorado market calendars are already live for 2026. Boulder runs Saturdays from **April 4-November 21, 2026** and Wednesdays from **May 6-October 7, 2026**. City Park runs Saturdays from **May 2-October 31, 2026**. This makes mid-April the right time for a fast audit and setup sprint before the busiest season.

## Funnel Plan

The wedge is a data audit, not generic farmers market education.

**Landing page promise:** Turn farmers market traffic into repeat online revenue. Upload your Shopify orders export and get a Market-to-Web Scorecard showing anonymous POS leakage, customer segments, product hooks, and your summer-to-fall growth opportunity.

**Free or low-cost diagnostic:** Market-to-Web Scorecard.

The vendor uploads a Shopify orders export and gets:

* Anonymous POS order rate.
* POS vs web AOV.
* Known market-only customers.
* Market + online customers.
* Online-only customers.
* Estimated upside from converting 5%, 10%, and 20% of anonymous or known market-only demand.
* Top markets or locations by volume and known POS order share.
* Top gateway products.
* A simple Market Capture Health grade.

**Primary CTA:** "Want the pilot plan built from your actual data?"

**Trust angle:** For the first public version, make privacy a feature. Shopify exports can contain emails, phones, names, addresses, and order metadata. The strongest version of the free scorecard processes the CSV in the browser so the file never leaves the vendor's device. If server upload is needed, use a clear privacy statement, delete files after processing, and avoid storing raw customer data unless the vendor explicitly agrees.

**Upsell path:**

1. Free scorecard creates the aha moment.
2. Paid audit turns the scorecard into a custom deck and summer-to-fall roadmap.
3. Capture Sprint implements the opt-in engine, email/SMS flows, staff scripts, signage copy, and dashboard.
4. Season Retainer keeps optimizing market performance every week.

## Repeatable Service Model

### Core Sales Thesis

Seasonal vendors are already doing the hard part: showing up, selling in person, and creating repeatable demand. The leak is that most of those customers stay anonymous, so the business cannot reliably follow up, drive online orders, or transition seasonal demand into off-season revenue.

**Positioning line:** I help seasonal vendors turn in-person market demand into owned customer lists, repeat online orders, and off-season revenue.

### Qualifying Questions For Future Clients

* Do you sell at farmers markets, pop-ups, fairs, food truck events, or seasonal markets?
* Do you use Shopify POS, Square, or another POS system?
* Do you also sell online, offer delivery, pickup, subscriptions, preorders, or bundles?
* Do you know what percentage of your POS buyers are anonymous?
* Do you currently collect email/SMS at the booth?
* Do you have explicit marketing consent for your existing customer list?
* What products are best for repeat purchase, preorder, or off-season delivery?
* What offer could you afford to test without hurting margin?
* Which markets are highest volume vs highest quality?
* Who on the team would actually prompt customers to scan, sign up, or pre-reserve?

### Proposal Sections To Reuse

* **Audit promise:** I will show you where market demand is leaking and what it could be worth to capture it.
* **Key metrics:** anonymous POS rate, known POS order share, POS vs online AOV, market-only vs online LTV, market + online customer value, offer opportunity, and pilot targets.
* **Implementation offer:** QR/sticker capture path, consent handling, email/SMS flow, source tags, first offer, dashboard, and weekly learning loop.
* **Attribution add-on:** member lookup/redeem flow that trains staff to attach returning list members to POS orders for stronger customer-level market attribution.
* **Client responsibilities:** data export/access, market rules, offer margin guardrails, print approval, staff prompt, and Klaviyo/Shopify access.
* **Pricing ladder:** audit, setup sprint, optional seasonal activation retainer.
* **Boundaries:** software costs, print costs, offer budget, legal/compliance review, and ad spend are separate.
* **Case study plan:** ask permission to use anonymized Casa Crobu stats once there are pilot results.

**Default source-of-record model:**

| Data Type | Source Of Record | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Customer profile | Shopify or Square customer directory | Use the POS/ecommerce customer ID as the durable customer record whenever possible. |
| Orders, revenue, products, discounts | POS/ecommerce platform | Do not recreate order/revenue truth in a spreadsheet. |
| Email/SMS consent and engagement | Email/SMS platform | Keep consent, unsubscribes, opens, clicks, replies, and flow performance in the messaging tool. |
| Market-list tags/segments | Both systems where possible | Sync tags/properties so customers are usable in both selling and messaging systems. |
| Dashboard | Reporting layer | Summarizes results; should not become the canonical source for customer/order data. |

## Compliance Guardrails

SMS capture must use compliant disclosure language wherever consent is collected. Klaviyo's guidance says disclosure language is required wherever SMS consent is collected, and its checklist includes message frequency, HELP/STOP instructions, privacy policy and terms links, message/data rates, and consent-not-required language. Treat this as operational guidance, not legal advice.
