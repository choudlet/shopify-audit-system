The Market Club system is live and doing its first job: turning people who see Casa Crobu into people Casa Crobu can reach again.

Since launch:

- **35 external people** have visited the Market Club page.
- **17 people** have joined the Market Club.
- That is a **nearly 50% signup rate** — nearly one out of every two visitors joined.
- **1 person** has already used the welcome offer.
- **12 people** agreed to receive email updates.
- **12 people** agreed to receive text updates.
- **7 people** agreed to receive both.

The $1,500 Phase 1B Capture System Build is working: the signup page, QR-code path, welcome offer, welcome email/text, customer list, market tracking, and basic reporting are in place.

The priority now is simple: **build the list.** The most urgent next move is to put a QR code on Casa Crobu product stickers. A sticker lets every package keep working after the market, giving customers another easy chance to join even when they did not scan at the booth.

## The most urgent next move: product stickers

Product stickers are the best next list-building tool because they are always with the customer. A booth QR only works while a person is standing at the booth. A sticker gives them a second chance to join when they get home, open the fridge, share the product, or decide they want the next offer.

### Start with one general product-sticker QR code

Start with a **general product-sticker QR code with no market built into the link**. The form will ask the shopper to choose their market or location, while the system still records that the signup came from a product sticker. This will be easier than doing per location stickers and managing multipel sticker inventory while capturing the main value which is enabling people to scan at home.

This is the right first version because it is simple to print at volume and can go on products sold at any market. Once there is enough scan volume, Casa Crobu can decide whether market-specific product stickers are worth the added print complexity.

### Reconfirm staff training

The customer path only works if staff make the ask and can handle the return visit. Before expanding to more markets, give every booth person the same three-step refresher:
