## What Phase 1 Is Meant To Do

Phase 1 is the launch setup. The goal is to get a clean capture system in place before the busiest part of summer so Casa Crobu can start turning anonymous market demand into owned email/SMS contacts.

## Main Difference Between Core And Recommended

The main difference is that **Core Setup** is a lighter first test, while **Recommended Setup** is the more complete version built to support stronger capture from the start.

Recommended Setup includes the pieces that make the pilot more useful long term:

* email + SMS capture from day one,
* list prep and consent review,
* cleaner Shopify/Klaviyo setup,
* stronger source tagging,
* and a fuller launch workflow for the team.

## My Recommendation

I recommend **Recommended Setup** if the goal is to build the system correctly before July and give Casa Crobu the best chance of growing both email and SMS capture this season.

If Casa Crobu wants the simplest possible starting point, **Core Setup** is still a valid lean launch option.

The **Member Lookup + POS Attribution Add-On** is optional. It is most valuable if Casa Crobu wants to connect returning market shoppers back to Shopify customer profiles at checkout and improve attribution on repeat market orders.
